# Vietnam Administrative Divisions Future Work

## Standard 3-Level Address System
If the business requirements change, we may transition to a standard 3-tier address lookup system:
1. Provinces (Tỉnh / Thành phố)
2. Districts (Quận / Huyện)
3. Wards (Phường / Xã)

This will require introducing a `District` model and updating references accordingly.
